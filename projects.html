PROJ-2EI4-PSU ANALOG / POWER

Bridge-Rectifier DC Power Supply

A 3 V, 10 mA DC supply designed by hand from a 120 V, 1 kHz AC source — transformer, full-wave bridge, and capacitive filter sized to a 0.2 V ripple budget. Simulation held 71.6 mV of ripple; the hardware build came in at 2.7 V and 9.1 mA, with the gap traced to real diode drops and contact resistance.
