About

I'm a second-year Electrical and Computer Engineering (Co-op) student at McMaster University in Hamilton, Ontario. My coursework and projects span embedded systems, analog and digital circuit design, and algorithms — but what I enjoy most is the full loop: calculating a design by hand, simulating it in LTSpice, building it on a breadboard, and verifying it with real instrumentation.

Recent work includes a LIDAR-style 3D spatial mapper built around a time-of-flight sensor and an ARM microcontroller, CMOS logic gates designed and characterized at the transistor level, and a BJT amplifier designed to a gain spec and validated against simulation. I document everything in IEEE-style technical reports.

I'm currently seeking co-op opportunities where I can contribute to hardware, embedded, or test engineering teams.
